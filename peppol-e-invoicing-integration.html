Requirements that may affect your business

CHECK APPLICABILITY

Belgian federal e-invoicing guidance

Belgium has required structured domestic B2B e-invoices for many VAT-liable businesses since 1 January 2026, subject to transaction and entity exceptions. A PDF alone is generally not a structured e-invoice.

Scope for this service: Confirm whether the Belgian domestic B2B mandate applies to each entity and transaction; other countries have different rules.
