STATUS: PAYMENTS DISABLED / NOT LIVE.
No payment provider, account or withdrawal method has been connected or tested.
The English /pay.html page, payment scripts and checkout styling are preserved.
The site must never collect or store card details itself.

STAGING COMPONENTS
- /pay.html — English project-payment page
- /assets/payment-config.js — currently approved: false, provider: "disabled"
- /assets/payments.js and /assets/payment-style.css
- Whop payment page-specific security rules in /_headers
- Hidden payment access in homepage, activated only when config is approved

OPTION A — WHOP
1. Create and verify an account; confirm the provider allows these services.
2. Configure an eligible payout account and complete all required checks.
3. Create a legitimate checkout for a documented project with clear terms.
4. Obtain the genuine Whop checkout Plan ID and set in payment-config.js:
       approved: true,
       provider: "whop",
       whopPlanId: "plan_YOUR_REAL_ID"
5. Test preview checkout and verify payouts, refunds, policies and iframe
   compatibility before any public launch.

OPTION B — CONTRA
1. Confirm you are eligible to use a hosted payment link for your work.
2. Create a genuine one-time project payment link.
3. Set in payment-config.js:
       approved: true,
       provider: "contra",
       contraPaymentUrl: "https://contra.com/YOUR_REAL_PAYMENT_LINK"
4. Buyers will be redirected to Contra's own hosted checkout.

IMPORTANT
- Whop / Contra approval, payout and eligibility cannot be assumed.
- The payer's bank or provider may apply 3-D Secure, account reviews,
  transaction limits, payout holds or other verification requirements.
- Comply with relevant business registration, invoice, tax and consumer laws.
- Do not misstate business registration, reviews or verified customer counts.
- Check the provider's current policies and technical documentation.
- Verify CSP integrations on the deployed /pay.html before enabling payments.
- Keep approved: false until the provider, business details, permitted
  service category and checkout have been verified and tested.
