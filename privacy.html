PRIVACY NOTICE

How enquiries are handled

Website contact: info@balkanagent.com. This site collects only information that you choose to submit in the project enquiry form: name, email, business, country, website, requested service and project details.

Purpose: to respond to your enquiry, discuss a possible business project and keep an appropriate record of the correspondence. Do not include passwords, private medical details or sensitive personal records in the form.

Delivery: if the form backend is enabled, enquiry data is transmitted to the configured email delivery provider and our business inbox. Provider details, processing roles, international transfers, retention arrangements and the legal operator's full contact information must be checked and completed before public launch.

Rights: you may request access, correction or deletion of your enquiry data by emailing us, subject to applicable law and retention requirements. You may also contact the relevant data protection authority.

Analytics: this version does not include non-essential analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies by default. Hosting systems may process technical connection logs needed to serve the site securely.

Note: this is a technical starter notice, not a jurisdiction-specific approved legal text. The site operator must finalise it before publication.
