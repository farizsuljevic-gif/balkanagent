# Contact – Adiya Signature Hotel

We offer two types of booking options:

**Refundable Rate:** You can cancel your reservation free of charge up to **7 days** before your scheduled arrival. If you cancel within **7 days** of arrival, a cancellation fee may apply.

**Non-Refundable Rate:** This option does not allow for free cancellation. If you cancel or do not show up, the full amount of the reservation will be charged.

Please review the rate conditions carefully when making your booking to select the option that best suits your needs.
